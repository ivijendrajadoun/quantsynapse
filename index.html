Guide 01

Position sizing: risk first

Position size connects the amount you are willing to risk with the distance between entry and stop-loss. A wider stop generally requires a smaller position to keep planned cash risk constant.

Risk = Account balance × Risk %

Lots ≈ Risk amount ÷ (Stop pips × Pip value per lot)

Open calculator →
Guide 02

Pip value and currency pairs

Most non-JPY pairs commonly use 0.0001 as a pip; JPY pairs commonly use 0.01. Cash pip value depends on size, quote currency, account currency and conversion rates.

Open calculator →
Guide 03

Reward-to-risk is not expectancy

A 2R target means planned gross reward is twice planned risk. It does not establish profitability; win rate, average win/loss, costs and execution all matter.

Open planner →
Guide 04

Drawdown recovery

The percentage gain needed to recover is larger than the percentage loss. A 20% drawdown requires a 25% gain to return to the starting balance.

Risk management basics

QQuantSynapse
HomeToolsGuidesSessionsAboutOpen tools ↗

1. Define planned risk before sizing the position

Decide how much capital you are prepared to risk under your plan before choosing a position size. A stop-loss may not guarantee the exact exit price during gaps or fast markets.

2. Confirm pip value and contract size

Check instrument specifications, lot convention and account-currency conversion. A pip value copied from another pair may produce an incorrect position size.

3. Include transaction costs

Spreads, commissions, swaps, slippage and conversion fees can change results. Simplified calculators may not include all costs.

4. Review concentration

Several trades may share exposure to the same currency or market factor. Adding risk amounts is a first check, not a correlation or stress model.

5. Treat estimates cautiously

Estimated win rates and reward-to-risk ratios do not guarantee future outcomes. Backtests can be affected by overfitting, costs and changing market regimes.

Pre-trade checklist

  • Have I defined the trade thesis and invalidation point?
  • Have I verified pip value, lot size and currency conversion?
  • Have I considered spreads, slippage and gaps?
  • Does this trade add too much correlated exposure?
